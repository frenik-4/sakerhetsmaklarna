## Scope
Whole marketing site (home, five service pages, text pages, contact, references, camera login). Mode: Persuade. Audience: Stockholm construction project managers/procurement first, acute shop/office cases second. Success: trust first, read as technically advanced and professional, phone one tap away.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Show the alarm system at work instead of describing it: the site opens on the live surface an operator would see (detectors in service, a motion event arriving with a verification photo, the alarm centre's decision). It refuses both the security-category default (dark photo hero with shield icons and three cards) and the previous construction-sign look (yellow fields, condensed caps everywhere).
OWN-WORLD: Deep navy (#071a33) control surfaces and brand blue #005cb9 as the working colour on cool white (#f6f8fb) ground; brand yellow #fddd00 only as the primary action and small live/status marks. Archivo at normal width, sentence case, tight tracking for headings; small uppercase micro-labels and tabular figures for data. 1px hairlines, 8–14px radii, layered soft shadows, faint technical grid on dark surfaces. Real company photos: detectors close up, sites.
STORY: Visitor sees what is rented and that it is a real monitored system (detectors, image verification, alarm centre, often live the same day, since 1999), trusts the contractors listed, then calls or requests a quote; service pages answer details with the quote form beside.
FIRST VIEWPORT: White header (logo, nav, "Logga in", phone text, yellow "Begär offert"). Full-bleed navy hero with faint grid: H1 and lead top left, "Ring 08-752 98 00" yellow primary and "Begär offert" secondary; to the right and spilling below, a wide system panel (labelled Exempel) with three panes: detectors list with status, incoming event with verification photo and timestamp, alarm-centre log with decision. Panel overlaps into the light section where customers follow.
FORM: "Teknisk och exakt", pinned by the user: seed: none (pinned) "Kör hela paketet" accepting the proposal "Visa systemet i arbete överst", "Gult blir en accent, inte en yta", "lugnare, mer exakt typografi", "Mörka partier", "'Så fungerar det' som ett flöde", "Produktbilder". Signature interaction: the hero event arrives once after load (detector pulses, photo appears, log line resolves) and the signal-flow diagram draws its path on scroll; reduced motion shows the settled state.
